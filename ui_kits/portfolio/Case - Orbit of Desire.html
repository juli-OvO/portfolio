Orbit of Desire

A sci-fi romance visual novel set on a botany research station. I designed and illustrated Uba Bobuba, a cheerful herbalist with a fragile constitution and questionable curiosity.

Plant care and dialogue run on the same variables.

Players manage plant growth alongside branching dialogue, affection meters, and multiple endings. The environmental variables feed both systems, so the temperature that stresses a specimen also affects the conversation.
