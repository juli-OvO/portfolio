## Description

A wall-mounted window with nothing behind it, lit through a patchwork of cast bioplastic. It senses room brightness and nearby viewers, and answers with green and ochre light.

## About

### Statement

Inspired by cottagecore and by memories of sitting beside large windows, this piece doesn't try to simulate a natural view: no blue sky, no realistic sunlight. Instead, green and ochre light, leaf-patterned muslin, and translucent bioplastic exaggerate my memory of what a window feels like. Rather than recreating nature, it reconstructs the sensation of filtered light, seasonal color, and quiet interior reflection.
