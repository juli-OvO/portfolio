## Description

An augmented-reality HUD for NASA's SUITS challenge, designed with the Brown–RISD student team to help astronauts navigate the lunar surface, work at points of interest, and return safely. In progress; full case study coming soon.

## About

### The challenge

Image left: nasalogo.png, risdlogo.png

NASA SUITS (Spacesuit User Interface Technologies for Students) asks student teams to design interfaces for astronauts working on the Moon during Artemis missions. Our AR team is designing the heads-up display inside the suit, working from the 2026–2027 mission description.

### Conditions we design for

Image right: nasa-suits-field-test.jpg — Image courtesy of NASA.

- Low, harsh lunar lighting, seen through passthrough AR
- Limited astronaut attention while walking and working
- Suit consumables, like oxygen and battery, and a safe return to the lander

### What we're exploring

- What's always visible, and what appears only when needed
- Navigation and route guidance
- Alerts for the suit, the body, and points of interest
- Where information lives: the HUD, the wrist, or the world

### My role

As VR/AR designer, I ran the color tests and contributed to the wireframes. Next, the team is collecting interaction references from aviation, automotive HUDs, diving gear, and industrial AR, and sketching a normal navigation state alongside an alert state before settling on one direction.
