### Depth layer — the story

**Walk me through what you did, in order** [designer]

i first made a sequencer with p5.js, then decided to make each button of the sequencer float, breaking the spatial arrangement, taking away definite control of the outcome. i also decided to add two lines xy axis so when buttons pass through them they make a sound too. i also added harmony lines and logs for both info and visual effect

**What did you decide, and why?** [designer]

i decided to scramble the visual order of sequencer buttons to experiement. it would be more fun to play with. also this means that it is not for standard music making but more like a toy. 

**What broke, or changed your mind?** [designer]

some liked the level of control some didnt. i decided to add a corresponding sequencer map on the bottom so user would know which one they clicked on. for more information and choices. 

**The craft — what are you proud of at close range?** [designer]

my first actual vibe coded peice. also i liked how the visuals turned out at last.
