Prosthetic Window

A wall-mounted window with nothing behind it. It reads the room's brightness and whether someone is standing close, then answers with green and ochre light through a cast bioplastic membrane.

01Overview

A wall-mounted window with no view behind it.

  • Windows as surfaces, not openings — read for weather, time of day, mood.
  • Green and filtered light come from my own association with an imagined greener outside, not a specific landscape.
  • Not realistic: no blue sky, no sunlight — an exaggeration of how a window feels.
  • On a wall, a stand-in for an opening the room doesn't have.

04Material notes
  • Needed translucent and uneven, so the light reads as filtered, not emitted — nothing off the shelf did it.
  • Cast bioplastic: water, glycerin, starch, gelatin — ratios varied batch by batch.
  • Plain sheets crack, so some are cast onto muslin to stay trimmable and hangable.
  • Pattern from pressed leaves (tannin heat transfer) plus liquid dye.
  • Each batch made one patch; patches joined into a membrane with seams left visible — it reads like old glass.

Material process
  • The schedule followed the material — sheets dried when they dried, a cracked batch cost a day.
  • Small patches kept failures cheap.
  • Planned an even membrane; ended up with a seamed one I preferred.
05System notes
  • Three systems — sensing, light, surface — each worked out separately.
Arduino circuit01

A photoresistor for room brightness and an ultrasonic sensor for presence. Those are the only two inputs; there are no controls.

LED light panel02

559 LEDs on aluminum-backed fabric. The reflective backing scatters the light, so the surface glows evenly instead of showing hundreds of points.

Bioplastic surface03

Cast sheets of water, glycerin, starch, and gelatin, some reinforced with muslin, patterned with leaf tannin and liquid dye, joined into a larger membrane and framed with wood.

06Rules
  • Two sensors, four rules.
  • No timer, no script — it only reacts to the room.
Light < 300LED panel on — it glows only once the room has gone dim.
Light > 300LED panel off — in daylight it reads as a surface.
Distance < 40 cmColor turns warmer and brighter.
Distance > 40 cmColor goes dimmer and colder.
  • Closer than 40 cm: hard to trigger on purpose.
  • Further: fired at anyone walking past.
  • 40 cm — only someone standing at the window turns it warm.
