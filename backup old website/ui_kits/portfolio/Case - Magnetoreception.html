01Overview

Birds can feel magnetic north. We can't.

Magnetoreception is the ability to read the inclination of the Earth's magnetic field — in birds, probably through proteins in the eye. Humans don't have it. I wanted to build a version you could wear and watch: a compass on the arm, with a wing as its output.

  • Magnetic fields, infrasound, UV — unsensed, so absent from how we picture the world.
  • A limit of the body, not of understanding.
  • So: make an added sense legible from outside the wearer.

03First test
  • No wing — the same heading delivered as vibration on the skin: felt, not shown.
  • Worn in the same electromagnetic noise (Wi-Fi, electronics) that interferes with real birds.
NorthStrong constant buzz
NortheastMedium buzz

SouthNo buzz
  • Dropped: a buzz is legible only to the wearer, and the piece had to read across a room.
  • The wing trades feeling your own sense for watching someone else's.
  • The haptic version is the one I'd build next.

  • Soldered, not breadboarded — jumper wires come apart on a piece that moves with the body.
  • Grass rope lashing: any unit unties and swaps in a minute, and the layout kept changing.
05Scope notes
Scope decision

Motion scope

Planned: fold, expand, and vertical motion. The servos lacked the torque, and the linkage to coordinate three axes was outgrowing the rest of the build.

Cut to vertical flapping, and spent the recovered time tuning range, speed, and easing until it read as flight.

IntendedFold, expand, and vertical motion — full flight simulation
BuiltVertical flapping only, tuned and smoothed
06Response logic
  1. Circuit powers up from the battery pack.
  2. The display shows the current bird — one of five migration routes — and the button cycles through them.
  3. The compass reads a heading, which is mapped onto that species' desired migration direction.
  4. Aligned: the wing sweeps in a wide sine-wave flap.
  5. Off course: the range and speed of the flap collapse.
CompassRaw heading, jittery
SmoothRolling average — raw values made the wing twitch
